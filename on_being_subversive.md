What I repeatedly see is that folks reveal their subversive position at the moment of exit. Look, if you have a position, you should hold it in every moment of the process. Before the ups and downs and through them and especially before any exit. If you add your personality in the last context frame, it has no weight. Weight comes from holding it through all frames, so does meaning. The good ones, the bad ones. The moments that confirm you, the moments that make you look ridiculous for holding your position. Not talking about stuborness or being stoic. More talking about standing for yourself, staying true to your essence and keep moving into the mirror that is you. If you don't do that for all frames except the last one, you keep no trace, no repetition, no mark. By holding it in the last frame, you are confirming that you didn't do it.
